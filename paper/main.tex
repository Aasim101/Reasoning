\begin{abstract}
Recent work bounds self-consistency by a \emph{modal ceiling} $\pmode$: the
fraction of items whose plurality answer is correct. Coverage can keep climbing
while selection plateaus---the \emph{identifiability gap}. The literature often
treats $\pmode$ as a property of a model on a benchmark. We ask whether
semantics-preserving elicitation changes the mode under \emph{sampled}
chain-of-thought on free-generation math, and whether spreading a fixed token
budget across configurations (configuration-diversified voting; CDV) can raise
that plateau. On a fully crossed grid over items, Qwen2.5\,\{1.5B,3B,7B\},
Llama-3.2-3B, and three core configurations with seed replicates, plug-in
$\pmode$ (maj@$N$) moves by more than 3\,pp on several cells---up to 13.3\,pp on
GSM-Symbolic---so ceilings are model--prompt properties (P4 supported). Mode
reorderings occur (mean 21.6\% of items) and concentrate on small-margin items,
but destructive transitions outpace corrective ones. Pre-registered token-matched
CDV does \textbf{not} beat single-configuration self-consistency (P5/F3
unsupported). Moment item\,$\times$\,config shares stay ${<}1\%$ despite ceiling
movement. Reported reasoning boundaries should be stated for a model--prompt
pair; configuration diversity alone is not a free lift of the modal plateau.
\end{abstract}

% ============================================================================
\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
% ============================================================================

Progress in language-model reasoning is increasingly mediated by how a model
\emph{selects} an answer under sampling. Compute-optimal test-time scaling bins
items by estimated difficulty~\citep{snell2024scaling}; adaptive
self-consistency stops when a vote looks decided~\citep{aggarwal2023adaptive,li2024esc};
and analyses that explain why extra samples stop helping bound selection by the
\emph{modal-hit rate} $\pmode$---the fraction of items whose plurality answer is
correct~\citep{brown2024monkeys,blendasc2025,certifiedsc2025}. More i.i.d.\ samples
estimate the mode more precisely; they cannot change which class is modal. If
elicitation changes the mode, then $\pmode$ is not a model constant.

That ``elicitation matters'' is established at the item level on
log-prob-scored suites~\citep{brittlebench2026,tee2026} and for unstable hardness
labels~\citep{hardunreached2026}. What these works do \textbf{not} examine is
whether sensitivity reaches the \textbf{mode} on \textbf{sampled} chain-of-thought
for free-generation math, where binomial noise is load-bearing, or whether
spreading budget across configurations can raise $\pmode$.

We ask three questions. \textbf{(Q1)} Does $\pmode$ move across
semantics-preserving elicitation configurations for the same item and model?
\textbf{(Q2)} If so, is the movement explained by \emph{reordering} of top answer
classes on small-margin items? \textbf{(Q3)} Can \emph{configuration-diversified
voting} (CDV) raise the plateau above single-configuration self-consistency at a
matched token budget, while temperature-diversified sampling does not?
Supporting measurement (Q4): item\,$\times$\,configuration structure in a crossed
decomposition, and difficulty-label transfer.

Answering these requires a fully crossed item\,$\times$\,model\,$\times$\,configuration
grid, independent-seed replicates within a reference configuration (noise floor),
and budget matching in \emph{output tokens} rather than sample count. We build
all three. Extended related work is in \cref{app:related}.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep1pt
  \item \textbf{$\pmode$ is configuration-relative (Q1).}
 Plug-in modal ceilings
    move by ${>}3$\,pp across core configurations on multiple
    model$\times$benchmark cells (Table~\ref{tab:main-acc}; \cref{fig:ceiling}).
  \item \textbf{Mode reordering (Q2).} Configurations reorder top answer classes
    (mean reorder rate $21.6\%$), concentrated on small-margin items; destructive
    transitions exceed corrective ones (\cref{fig:reorder}).
  \item \textbf{CDV does not raise the plateau (Q3; licensed negative).}
 At
    matched completion-token budgets, CDV fails to beat single-configuration
    self-consistency on this grid (\cref{fig:cdv}; P5/F3 unsupported).
  \item \textbf{Supporting measurement (Q4).} Moment item\,$\times$\,config shares
    are tiny (${<}1\%$) despite P4 movement; transferred-difficulty allocation
    often retains less gain than configuration-aware allocation
    (\cref{fig:variance,fig:transfer}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{What is new.} We do not claim first discovery of prompt sensitivity
\citep{brittlebench2026,tee2026,prompteval2024,sclar2024formatspread}. Our claim is
that sensitivity reaches the \emph{mode} that caps self-consistency under sampled
reasoning, measured with a design that can falsify both the ceiling claim and the
CDV intervention.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
% ============================================================================

A single sampling corpus supports every quantity offline: no training and no
extra generation for CDV.

\paragraph{Notation.}
For each cell $(i,m,c,s)$ we draw $N$ chains, extract and canonicalise answers,
and record $k_{imcs}$ correct. Write $\phat_{imcs}=k/N$ and the Haldane logit
$z=\log\frac{k+1/2}{N-k+1/2}$. An \emph{elicitation configuration} perturbs
everything except mathematical content. Core set $\{c_0,c_1,c_2\}$:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt
  \item $c_0$: neutral system (``helpful assistant''), zero-shot CoT, $T{=}0.8$;
  \item $c_1$: expert-mathematician system prompt; else as $c_0$;
  \item $c_2$: family-minimal / default-system rendering (audited per family).
\end{itemize}
Parse ($c_3$), few-shot, and temperature axes are separate; $c_5$ appears only in
temperature-diversified SC (B4).

\paragraph{Modal ceiling.}
Plurality class $a^\star$ over $N$ samples; $\pmode(m,c)$ is the fraction of
items with $a^\star$ correct. Gap: $\mathrm{pass@}N-\pmode$. We measure
$\pmode$ ourselves~\citep{brown2024monkeys,bay2026modal}.

\paragraph{Supporting GLMM / moments.}
Crossed binomial GLMM following~\citet{tee2026,prompteval2024}; draft artefacts
use a method-of-moments decomposition on Haldane logits with
\texttt{--skip-glmm} (camera-ready reruns VB-GLMM). Seed replicates at $c_0$
supply the noise floor and $r_{mm}$ for disattenuation
$\rdis=\rho_{\mathrm{raw}}/r_{mm}$. Hard-subset Jaccard across configs vs.\ seeds
measures excess instability.

\paragraph{CDV.}
Split token budget $B$ evenly across $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{use}}=\{c_0,c_1,c_2\}$,
pool answer classes, return plurality (\cref{alg:cdv}). Compare to SC and
temperature-diversified SC at matched tokens. Primary test P5/F3: plug-in
$\pmode(\mathrm{CDV})$ vs.\ $\pmode(c_0)$.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Configuration-Diversified Voting (CDV)}
\label{alg:cdv}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require item $i$, model $m$, token budget $B$, set $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{use}}$
\State $b \gets B / |\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{use}}|$
\State $P \gets \emptyset$
\For{$c \in \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{use}}$}
  \State draw chains from $(i,m,c)$ until cumulative output tokens reach $b$; add classes to $P$
\EndFor
\State \Return $\arg\max_{a}\#\{a\in P\}$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Models.}
\texttt{Qwen/Qwen2.5-\{1.5B,3B,7B\}-Instruct}~\citep{qwen25,qwen2} and
\texttt{meta-llama/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct}. Inference with Hugging Face
\texttt{float16}, \texttt{device\_map=auto}, on $1$--$2\times$ NVIDIA T4 (16\,GB).
Precision control: NF4 4-bit on 1.5B/3B MATH-500 only.

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}
MATH-500~\citep{math500} (headline; 200-item subsample), GSM8K~\citep{cobbe2021gsm8k}
(secondary; contamination risk~\citep{zhang2024gsm1k}), and GSM-Symbolic
main/p2~\citep{mirzadeh2025gsmsymbolic} (150 items; stress test). Fixed item-index
files are shipped with the harness.

\paragraph{Sampling.}
Strategy \texttt{configured\_sampling}, $N{=}24$ chains/item (O2: $N{=}64$ on
Qwen2.5-3B$\times$MATH-500$\times\{c_0..c_3\}$), $T{=}0.8$, $\mathrm{top\_p}{=}0.95$,
\texttt{max\_new\_tokens}${=}1024$. Seeds $\{0,1,2\}$ at $c_0$ for Qwen ladder
noise floors; headline table uses seed~$0$. Answers: extract then symbolic
canonicalisation; extraction-failure rates logged in T1/T2.

\paragraph{Corpus.}
66 complete graded runs in the fp16/4-bit split ($61$ fp16 + $6$ 4-bit
directories; \texttt{tab1\_setup.csv}). Analysis:
\texttt{python -m src.analysis.paper} $\rightarrow$ Figures~F1--F8 / Tables~T1--T7
in \texttt{paper\_artefacts/}. Draft build used \texttt{--skip-glmm --n-bootstrap 0};
see Limitations.

\paragraph{Pre-registered predictions.}
P4: $\pmode$ range ${>}3$\,pp across $c_0$--$c_2$. P5: token-matched CDV $>$ SC
and $>$ temperature SC. P1: item$\times$config share large vs.\ null. P7: 4-bit
leaves shares/$\rdis$ nearly unchanged. Falsifiers F3/F4/F1 as in
\texttt{docs/METHOD\_SPEC.md}.

\subsection{Variance, transfer, precision, downstream (Q4)}
\label{sec:results-support}

Moment decomposition (\cref{fig:variance}; Table~T3): item and model dominate;
item\,$\times$\,config share is $0.48\%$ (GSM8K) and $0.13\%$ (MATH-500), so

GSM-Symbolic lacks a fully crossed four-model design (no Llama), so its variance
block is omitted.

\paragraph{Precision (P7).}
On MATH-500, 4-bit drops mean $\pmode$ sharply vs.\ fp16
($\Delta\approx -16$\,pp pooled in T7) while item\,$\times$\,config share moves by
${<}0.2$\,pp. $\rdis$ is missing on the 4-bit arm in this draft, so P7 is
\textbf{unsupported} pending camera-ready reliability (\cref{fig:precision}).

\paragraph{Downstream (P6).}
Table~T6: where adaptive gains are reportable, transferring difficulty from
another configuration often retains ${<}1$ of the aware-allocation gain
(sometimes negative on 1.5B GSM8K; $\approx 0.94$ on 3B GSM8K).

\section{Analysis}
\label{sec:analysis}
% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Susceptible items.}
Reorderings concentrate on small-margin items (\cref{fig:reorder}). Large-margin
items almost never change mode under our core prompts.

\paragraph{Why CDV fails despite reordering.}
Destructive transitions ($6.4\%$) exceed corrective ($4.0\%$): configuration
changes often promote a wrong class into the plurality. Diversifying elicitation
without a verifier therefore need not raise $\pmode$.

\paragraph{Scale and shift.}
P4 spreads are non-monotone in size (3B GSM8K $0.5$\,pp vs.\ 1.5B/7B $6.5$\,pp).
Moment item\,$\times$\,config shares stay ${<}1\%$. GSM-Symbolic shows the
largest spreads (up to $13.3$\,pp); we treat it as a ceiling stress test, not a
second GLMM.

\paragraph{Identifiability gap remains.}
Oracle-coverage / oracle-best-single rows in Table~T5 remain well above SC and
CDV; configuration diversification alone does not close the gap of
\citet{bay2026modal}.

\paragraph{Compatibility with small interaction shares.}
Large $\pmode$ gaps with tiny item\,$\times$\,config variance shares are
compatible when configuration reorders mass near the decision boundary rather
than inducing a large crossed random effect.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
% ============================================================================

\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt
  \item \textbf{Scale.} Models fit on $\le 2\times$16\,GB GPUs; frontier behaviour
    may differ.
  \item \textbf{Configuration sample.} Three core prompts are not a census;
    estimates are conditional on $\{c_0,c_1,c_2\}$.
  \item \textbf{Semantics preservation} is argued via axes that avoid rewriting
    the problem; residual task shift is possible.
  \item \textbf{Finite $N$.} $N{=}24$ (mostly); seed arm corrects noise only as
    far as replicates allow. O2 replaces some MATH-500 3B cells with $N{=}64$.
  \item \textbf{Benchmarks.} MATH-500/Symbolic lead; GSM8K is secondary
    (contamination).
  \item \textbf{Canonicalisation.} Symbolic checker errors enter $\pmode$; we
    log extraction failures.
  \item \textbf{Draft statistics.} Checked-in artefacts used
    \texttt{--skip-glmm --n-bootstrap 0}. Camera-ready should rerun with GLMM and
    bootstrap before treating CIs / P1 nulls as final.
  \item \textbf{P7.} 4-bit $\rdis$ unavailable in draft; share gate alone is
    insufficient to call P7 supported.
\end{itemize}

% ============================================================================
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ============================================================================

On a fully crossed sampled-CoT grid, the self-consistency ceiling is not a model
constant: plug-in $\pmode$ moves by more than three points across
semantics-preserving elicitation configurations on several
model$\times$benchmark cells, with the largest spreads on GSM-Symbolic.
Mode reordering is real and small-margin-concentrated, but destructive
transitions dominate corrective ones. Pre-registered configuration-diversified
voting does not raise the modal plateau above single-configuration
self-consistency at matched tokens. Practitioners should report ceilings for a
\emph{model--prompt pair}; diversifying prompts without verification is not a
substitute for depth or for better selection.

\section*{Impact Statement}
This work studies evaluation and test-time aggregation for open-weight math
reasoning models. Potential impacts are improved honesty in reporting reasoning
benchmarks and more careful use of difficulty labels in curricula. We do not
release new high-risk capabilities; experiments are inference-only on public
benchmarks. Misuse risk is low relative to generative systems; the main risk is
over-interpreting negative CDV results as ``prompts never matter'' despite P4.
